About Me

Burhanuddien Robbani, S.P.

Burhanuddien Robbani, S.P.

Hello, I'm still learning and contributing in mangrove rehabilitation, geospatial mapping, and coastal community empowerment. Over the past few years I've had the chance to learn hands-on through the national mangrove restoration program, including with the Peatland and Mangrove Restoration Agency and M4CR–the Ministry of Forestry.

Detail-oriented spatial analyst with 3 years of experience supporting large-scale coastal ecosystem projects through UAV mapping and GIS planning. Proficient in translating drone imagery and hydrological data into clear, actionable blueprints for implementing groups. A collaborative team player with a strong eagerness to learn, refine technical skills, and support data-driven decision-making in sustainable forestry and blue carbon initiatives. — I try to connect knowledge with real action, though there's still much to learn, for the sake of Indonesia's coastal ecosystems.

Competencies

Skills & Specialization

🌱

Mangrove and Coastal Rehabilitation

Equipment & Software: GNSS/GPS Receiver (Garmin/Trimble), ArcGIS Field Maps, KoboToolbox/ODK, Measuring Tape/Haglof

  • Conducting high-precision field surveys and Ground Truthing (sample point validation) using GNSS instruments and ArcGIS Field Maps.
  • Spatial design and rehabilitation zonation planning based on Land Suitability analysis.
  • Monitoring Survival Rate and periodic inventory of mangrove stand health.
  • Identification of coastal flora taxonomy and design of appropriate ecological restoration interventions.
🗺️

GIS and Remote Sensing

Equipment & Software: ArcGIS Pro, QGIS, and GEE

  • Acquisition and pre-processing of medium-resolution satellite imagery (Sentinel-2/Landsat 8 & 9) using cloud computing via Google Earth Engine (GEE), including atmospheric correction and cloud masking.
  • Feature extraction and mangrove canopy density classification using Vegetation Indices (NDVI/MDI) and Machine Learning algorithms (e.g., Random Forest/CART) in ArcGIS Pro/QGIS.
  • Multitemporal Land Use/Cover Change (LULC) analysis to monitor deforestation dynamics and mangrove ecosystem degradation during the period 2015-2025.
  • Advanced cartographic design and interactive spatial data visualization to support decision-making (Decision Support) in mangrove rehabilitation strategy.
🛸

UAV / Drone Operation & Mapping

Equipment & Software: DJI Mavic 2 / Air 2S, DJI Phantom 4, Mission Planning App (Dronelink), Agisoft Metashape, Pix4D

  • Planning and executing autonomous flight mapping missions for high-resolution coastal spatial data acquisition.
  • Photogrammetry processing to produce ultra-resolution Orthomosaic and Digital Elevation/Surface Model (DEM/DSM).
  • Extraction and classification of Point Cloud data for 3D reconstruction of mangrove landscape models.
  • Monitoring canopy condition and micro-scale coastal vegetation health based on photogrammetry data.
👥

Community Engagement & Social Forestry

Equipment & Software: Google Workspace, Canva, Participatory Mapping Approach (PGIS)

  • Facilitation and institutional assistance for Social Forestry (KTH/LPHD) in sustainable mangrove forest management.
  • Local community capacity building through technical training and ecological education.
  • Project management, preparation of progress/performance reports, and production of comprehensive project visual documentation.
  • Initiation and development of Alternative Livelihood schemes based on Non-Timber Forest Products (NTFP) utilization of the mangrove ecosystem.
