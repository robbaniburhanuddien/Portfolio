Large-Scale Mangrove Planting

Rehabilitation 2025

Restored 500+ ha of coastal mangrove through ecology-based planting and spatial monitoring.

Large-scale planting across degraded North Sumatra coastal ponds, combining GIS-based site selection, hydrological assessment, and community-led implementation. Delivered 500+ ha planted with 10K+ seedlings and first-year maintenance built into the work plan.

  • 500+ ha kawasan pesisir direhabilitasi
  • 10K+ bibit ditanam + pemeliharaan tahun pertama
  • Koordinasi pokmas + pemerintah daerah
Context
North Sumatra's coastal belt has lost mangrove cover to pond conversion and abrasion. The national restoration programme targeted degraded ponds and open mudflats where hydrology still allowed replanting.
Roles
Planned planting layouts and verified field implementation as GIS/technical facilitator; coordinated with community groups (pokmas) and local government on site selection and maintenance.
Process
Land-suitability analysis in GIS, hydrological survey to decide water-gate interventions, UAV orthophoto baseline, GNSS ground-truthing, and survival monitoring on permanent plots.
Impact
500+ ha planted and maintained in the project area, with 10K+ seedlings and survival checks feeding the provincial 831 ha programme target.
